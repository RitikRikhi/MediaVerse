# DSA MediaVerse AI — Field Operations Manual

**Target Event:** SAVISKAR 2026 (October 28–29, 2026)  
**Organization:** DSA Media Crew, CGC University

---

## 1. Quick Start by Role

### For Media Coordinators (Priya Patel)
1. **Event Overview:** Monitor the live coordinator dashboard for overdue tasks and bottlenecks.
2. **Assignments:** Create coverage assignments for each stage (Inauguration, Rock Concert, Robo-Wars, Star Night).
3. **Crew Assignment:** Assign photographers and videographers to specific sessions.
4. **Drive Provisioning:** Go to **Google Drive Setup** and click **Provision SAVISKAR Folders** to establish standard directories.
5. **Backups:** Download regular JSON / CSV backups of all logged footage and assignments.

### For Photographers & Videographers (Rohan Singh & Vikram Malhotra)
1. Open **Field Crew Portal** on your smartphone or tablet.
2. Review your assigned time slot, stage location, and expected deliverables.
3. During shooting, tap each shot in the **Shot Checklist** as it is captured.
4. After coverage, upload files to your designated Google Drive folder.
5. Click **Register Uploaded Media**, paste the Drive link, enter the file name, and click **Register**.
6. The assignment will automatically notify the video editors that fresh footage is ready!

### For Video Editors (Simran Kaur)
1. Open **Editor Studio**.
2. Browse registered raw clips and audio stems in the **Registered Footage Bank**.
3. Select your assigned editing project to read the **Creative Brief** and aspect ratio requirements (e.g., 9:16 vertical reel).
4. Export your cut (e.g., `v1 Rough Cut`) and upload to the Review Versions Drive folder.
5. Click **Submit Edit Version**, paste the Drive link, and add change notes.
6. The status automatically advances to `IN_REVIEW`.

### For QC Reviewers & Leadership (Dr. Neha Gupta & Aman Verma)
1. Open **Review & Approvals**.
2. Select the version awaiting review.
3. Preview the cut or open the Google Drive file.
4. Add timecode-specific notes (e.g. at 12.4s: "add sound effect").
5. Click **Approve Deliverable** or **Request Revision**.
6. If approved as final, check "Mark as Final Deliverable" to advance status to `DELIVERED`.

---

## 2. Emergency Fallback Procedures
- **No Internet in Main Stage / Tech Arena:** Field crew can keep shooting and record shot progress locally; paste Drive links once connected to campus Wi-Fi.
- **AI Service Rate Limited / Down:** All assignment creation, checklists, media registration, editing, and approvals work 100% without AI. The AI assistant automatically switches to the built-in deterministic rule engine.
- **Google Drive API Not Configured:** The system accepts any valid Google Drive share URL directly.
